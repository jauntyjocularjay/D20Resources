# Yautja

The **Yautja**, (pronounced Ya-OOT-ja or YOWT-ja), known colloquially as the **Predators** or **Hunters**, are an extraterrestrial species characterized by their hunting of other dangerous species for sport and honor, including humans. The Yautja are a sentient humanoid race that breathe an atmosphere similar to that of Earth's but possess a level of technological advancement far in excess of anything available to humans. The Predators stalk and kill their prey using a combination of highly advanced technology, such as active camouflage and energy weapons, combined with comparatively primitive traditional weapons, such as blades, spears and nets. The Predators often ritualistically mutilate their prey and usually claim a trophy from their kills. Capable of interstellar travel in star ships, the Predators have hunted on Earth for centuries and have also had prior contact with the Engineers.

Aside from their repeated conflicts with humans, the Predators have notably been associated with Xenomorph XX121, known to them as *kainde amedha* ("hard meat"), whom they revere as perhaps the worthiest of all prey. They have been known to deliberately breed the creatures from captive Queens in order to hunt them, often as part of initiation rituals for young Predators.

## Physiology

Predators are bipedal humanoids, physically distinguishable from humans by their greater height, the long, hair-like appendages on their heads (nicknamed dreadlocks), clawed hands, reptilian skin and their faces, which feature arthropod-like mandibles and no visible nose. The biological purpose of the distinctive mandibles is unclear – some have proposed they may be used in reproduction or mating rituals. Yautja also use them to convey emotions; for example, flared mandibles apparently signify anger or surprise, mandibles clicking together can signify interest or curiosity, and Yautja have even been said to "grin" with them. Predators have also been known to employ their mandibles as weapons, using them to inflict grievous bite wounds on their opponent. As well as the fleshy dreadlocks around the side of the head, some Predators have also been seen to possess sparse quills on their cheeks and above the eyes.

While generally uniform, each Yautja's physical appearance includes a number of subtle variations, akin to human genetic diversity. Similarly, while Predator heights vary, they are typically over 7 feet tall, although some have been known to grow to 8 feet or even taller. Despite this, shorter individuals have been recorded, who stood the height of a typical human. These individuals are unusual and their smaller height the subject of ridicule in Yautja society. The species' reptile-like skin can range in color from light to dark, be mottled or clear, and can appear dry or moist and clammy.

## Culture

Yautja culture centers on the ritualistic hunting of other dangerous lifeforms, and this practice appears to be the foundation of their very society. Predators will travel huge distances – even across entire galaxies – in order to face opponents they consider a worthy challenge, and may also kidnap and transport prey across similar distances to bring such victims to a hunting ground of their choice. Advanced equipment marks the status of the Hunters and must be earned, not simply awarded. The stronger and more experienced the hunters, the better and more advanced gear they can have. In some tribes, many Un-blooded Yautja are not allowed to have cloaking devices or acid-proof equipment until they become Blooded.

Defeat in a Hunt is apparently a cause of great shame to the Yautja, and often leads to the individual committing honorable suicide, typically through the detonation of their Wrist Gauntlet's Self-Destruct Device. Upon their death, a hunting Yautja's spacecraft will return to the species homeworld on automatic pilot so that a record of the individual's Hunt, recorded through their bio-helmet, may be returned to its kin.

The Yautja have a Code of Honor during the hunt that it expects all members of the species to adhere to on pain of not being seen as a "true Yautja" and therefore subject to being destroyed. Therefore, most Yautja are moulded around that code, being ruthless but honorable, with the exception of Bad Bloods. Aggression and arrogance seems to be innate in Hunters, especially young males.

## Names

The Yautja society appears to be a heavily gendered one; even the names of the Predators tend to have masculine and feminine connotations, similar to many romantic language such as spanish. Male names often end with 'e' (i.e: Dachande, Tichinde, Skemte, Nakande, ect.) while female names tend to end with 'i' (i.e: Vagouti, Hashori). Dachande even nicknames Machiko Noguchi "Da'dtou-di", which is the feminine form of that name.

Some names, such as Lar'nix'va, Bakuub, and Warkha do not fit into this naming trend however. This could be because the names are unisex or that different clans have different naming conventions.

## Yautja Racial Traits

**Species Type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Medium  
**Base Speed:** 40 ft.  
**Languages:** Yautja; bonus languages may include English, Japanese, Spanish, Engineer dialects, Weyland-Yutani technical codes, or other campaign-relevant languages.

## Ability Score Modifiers

Yautja are physically superior to humans, highly resilient, and trained from youth in observation, stalking, and combat.

### Standard Yautja PC Modifiers

| Ability      | Modifier |
| ------------ | -------: |
| Strength     | +4       |
| Dexterity    | +2       |
| Constitution | +4       |
| Intelligence | +0       |
| Wisdom       | +2       |
| Charisma     | -2       |

**Reasoning:**  
Yautja are brutally strong, fast, durable, and perceptive, but their alien bearing, mandibles, hunting customs, and predatory demeanor make ordinary social interaction with humans difficult.

For more politically refined or diplomatic Yautja, Charismatic Hero remains viable because class talents and skills can overcome the racial penalty.

## Core Species Traits

## Powerful Build

A Yautja counts as one size category larger whenever doing so would be beneficial for opposed checks related to strength, grappling, lifting, carrying, pushing, or resisting bull rush/trip attempts.

This does **not** increase weapon size by default unless the GM allows Yautja-forged weapons to be treated as oversized.

## Dense Bone and Muscle

Yautja gain:

- **Natural Armor +2**
- **Damage Reduction 2/-**
- **+2 racial bonus on Fortitude saves**
- **+4 racial bonus on checks to resist disease, poison, radiation, and environmental extremes**

At **Blooded rank or higher**, their Damage Reduction improves through classification benefits.

## Hunter’s Leap

Yautja gain a **+4 racial bonus on Jump checks** and may make standing jumps as if they had a running start.

They also reduce falling damage by **10 feet** as long as they are conscious and able to react.

## Predator Climber

Yautja gain a **+2 racial bonus on Climb, Balance, and Move Silently checks**.

They are comfortable fighting from trees, rooftops, gantries, ship superstructures, cliff faces, industrial scaffolds, and ruined architecture.

## Infrared Vision

Yautja naturally perceive primarily through the infrared spectrum.

They gain:

- **Darkvision/Infrared Vision 60 ft.**
- **+2 racial bonus on Spot checks against living warm-blooded creatures**
- They suffer a **-2 penalty on Spot checks** to distinguish fine visual detail, writing, holographic displays, or objects of similar temperature without a bio-mask or visual aid.

## Bio-Mask Interface

Yautja may use a bio-mask to expand their senses.

A functioning bio-mask can provide:

- Low-light vision
- Enhanced infrared filtering
- Ultraviolet spectrum
- Magnification
- Target tagging
- Atmospheric analysis
- Translation assistance
- Recording of hunts
- Weapon synchronization with plasma caster or smart weapons

Without a bio-mask, a Yautja can still function, but loses many advanced visual and tactical options.

## Mandibles and Claws

A Yautja has natural weapons.

**Claw Strike:** 1d4 slashing damage  
**Mandible Bite:** 1d6 piercing damage

A Yautja is always considered armed for purposes of threatening squares and making attacks of opportunity. However, Yautja usually consider killing worthy prey with only natural attacks either deeply honorable or brutally personal, depending on clan custom.

## Alien Presence

Yautja suffer a **-4 penalty on Bluff, Diplomacy, Gather Information, and Perform checks** when dealing openly with ordinary humans unfamiliar with their species.

They gain a **+4 racial bonus on Intimidate checks** against humans, synthetics, corporate soldiers, colonists, and other beings who understand the threat they represent.

## Hunter’s Patience

Yautja gain a **+2 racial bonus on Hide, Move Silently, Listen, Spot, Survival, and Search checks** when stalking a chosen target.

Once per encounter, after observing a target for at least 3 rounds, a Yautja may gain a **+1 insight bonus on attack rolls** against that target for the rest of the encounter.

This bonus increases with classification.

## Code of the Hunt

Most Yautja follow a strict honor code. The exact code varies by clan, but usually includes:

- Do not kill helpless prey.
- Do not kill the pregnant, sick, unarmed, or those incapable of meaningful resistance.
- Claim trophies only from worthy prey.
- Do not waste a worthy death.
- Do not abandon the hunt through cowardice.
- Do not dishonor the clan through reckless stupidity.
- Bad Bloods are to be hunted.

A Yautja who violates the Code may lose access to clan resources, honor-based prestige classes, higher-ranked equipment, and social standing.

## Yautja Heroic Class Access

Yautja society is diverse. All heroic classes are open.

| Heroic Class     | Yautja Expression                                              |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Strong Hero      | Berserkers, duelists, trophy-takers, heavy melee hunters       |
| Fast Hero        | Ambushers, rooftop stalkers, scout-hunters, blade dancers      |
| Tough Hero       | Juggernauts, xeno-hunters, shield-bearers, survivalists        |
| Smart Hero       | Techwrights, ship engineers, weapon makers, trap specialists   |
| Dedicated Hero   | Trackers, judges, ritualists, beast-handlers, clan lorekeepers |
| Charismatic Hero | Clan envoys, pack leaders, honor-speakers, fearsome commanders |

This means a Yautja PC is not automatically “just a Strong/Tough Hero.” A Smart Hero Yautja might be a master of cloaking systems and alien engineering. A Dedicated Hero might be a sacred hunter who interprets omens and judges honor. A Charismatic Hero might lead hunts, negotiate clan alliances, or terrify prey into mistakes.

## Yautja Classification by Character Level

These classifications represent **social rank, hunting maturity, reputation, and equipment access**. They do not replace heroic classes. A Yautja still levels normally in Strong Hero, Fast Hero, Tough Hero, etc.

## Classification Table

| Character Level | Classification | Status                        |
| --------------: | -------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 1–3             | Unblooded      | Initiate hunter               |
| 4–5             | Young-Blooded  | Proven but still watched      |
| 6–7             | Blooded        | Full hunter                   |
| 8–10            | Elite          | Veteran trophy-taker          |
| 11–13           | Pack Leader    | Commander of hunts            |
| 14–17           | Clan Leader    | Ruler, warlord, master hunter |
| 18–20           | Elder          | Living legend                 |

## Classification Benefits

These benefits are gained automatically when the Yautja reaches the appropriate total character level, assuming the character remains in good standing with the Code of the Hunt.

## Levels 1–3: Unblooded

Unblooded Yautja are young, ambitious, dangerous, and often reckless. They are usually denied the most advanced equipment until they survive a worthy initiation.

**Benefits:**

- Hunter’s Patience insight bonus: +1
- Natural Armor +2
- Damage Reduction 2/-
- May use basic Yautja weapons
- +1 Reputation bonus among Yautja youths or same hunting pack

**Typical Equipment Access:**

**Allowed:**

- Wrist blades
- Combi-stick
- Ceremonial dagger
- Net launcher, if clan permits
- Basic medkit
- Basic bio-mask
- Light hunting armor

**Restricted or forbidden:**

- Full cloaking device
- Plasma caster
- Acid-proof armor
- Self-destruct device
- Clan ship command access
- Advanced xenomorph-hunting gear

**Roleplay Notes:**

Unblooded Yautja are eager to prove themselves. They may be brave, impulsive, arrogant, or desperate to earn recognition. Their first serious hunt defines their future.

## Levels 4–5: Young-Blooded

Young-Blooded Yautja have survived a proving hunt or major initiation. They are now recognized as hunters, though still beneath true Blooded status.

**Benefits:**

- Hunter’s Patience insight bonus improves to +2
- +2 bonus on Survival checks to track wounded prey
- +1 bonus on Initiative checks
- May select one Yautja Honor Mark

**Honor Mark Examples:**

**Choose one:**

- **Hard Meat Scar:** +2 bonus on saves against acid or xenomorph-related fear effects.
- **Manhunter’s Mark:** +1 bonus on attack rolls against humans after observing them for 3 rounds.
- **Silent Taker:** +2 bonus on Hide and Move Silently while using elevated terrain.
- **Trophy Claimant:** +2 bonus on Intimidate when displaying trophies.
- **Blood Trial Survivor:** Once per day, reroll a failed Fortitude save.

**Typical Equipment Access:**

**May gain limited access to:**

- Cloaking device
- Improved bio-mask
- Netgun
- Smart disc/fan disc
- Light plasma weapon, with supervision or restriction
- Limited acid-resistant treatment

**Roleplay Notes:**

Young-Blooded hunters are dangerous because they have tasted victory but have not yet mastered restraint.

## Levels 6–7: Blooded

A Blooded Yautja is a full adult hunter. They have survived meaningful prey, claimed worthy trophies, and earned the right to carry more respected equipment.

**Benefits:**

- Natural Armor improves to +3
- Damage Reduction improves to 3/-
- Hunter’s Patience insight bonus remains +2
- +2 bonus on Fortitude saves against poison, disease, radiation, and acid exposure
- +2 Reputation bonus among hunters
- May craft or maintain personal Yautja hunting gear with appropriate Craft checks

**Blooded Rite:**

At this rank, the Yautja should have completed a defining hunt. Common examples:

- Slaying a xenomorph
- Surviving a hunt against armed humans
- Defeating a rival hunter
- Recovering lost clan technology
- Killing a monstrous alien predator
- Escaping a hostile world with trophy and honor intact

**Typical Equipment Access:**

**Allowed:**

- Cloaking device
- Plasma caster
- Smart disc/fan disc
- Full bio-mask suite
- Acid-resistant armor
- Wrist computer
- Self-destruct device, if clan permits
- Personal ship access, but not command of major vessels

**Roleplay Notes:**

This is the baseline “Predator” most humans fear: patient, armed, honorable, and deadly.

## Levels 8–10: Elite

Elites are veterans who have survived many hunts. They are no longer merely proving themselves; they are examples to others.

This is the first classification where **Yautja prestige classes should become available**.

**Benefits:**

- Natural Armor improves to +4
- Damage Reduction improves to 4/-
- Hunter’s Patience insight bonus improves to +3
- +2 bonus on Initiative
- +2 bonus on Intimidate and Survival
- May command Unblooded or Young-Blooded hunters during a sanctioned hunt
- May modify or personalize advanced gear

**Elite Privilege:**

An Elite may choose one:

- **Ghost Hunter:** +2 bonus on Hide while cloaked; reduce cloak distortion penalties.
- **Hard Meat Veteran:** Acid damage is reduced by 5 points per exposure/attack.
- **Duelist of the Old Ways:** +1 bonus to attack and damage with wrist blades, combi-stick, spear, or ceremonial blade.
- **Plasma Adept:** +1 bonus on attack rolls with plasma caster or shoulder-mounted energy weapons.
- **Trophy-Lord:** +2 bonus to Reputation and Intimidate when trophies are visible.

**Typical Equipment Access:**

**Allowed:**

- Customized bio-mask
- Superior cloak
- Personalized plasma caster
- Specialized xenomorph-hunting armor
- Ceremonial trophies integrated into armor
- Access to restricted hunting grounds
- Command of small hunting craft

**Roleplay Notes:**

Elites are where individual legend begins. Their armor, weapons, and trophies should tell a story.

## Levels 11–13: Pack Leader

Pack Leaders command hunts. They are responsible not only for victory, but for the honor and survival of those beneath them.

**Benefits:**

- Natural Armor improves to +5
- Damage Reduction improves to 5/-
- Hunter’s Patience insight bonus remains +3
- Allies within 30 ft. gain +1 morale bonus on attack rolls against a target the Pack Leader has marked or wounded
- +4 Reputation bonus among hunters
- May issue honor challenges, approve Blooding rites, and judge lesser breaches of the Code

**Pack Command:**

Once per encounter as a move action, the Pack Leader may designate one visible enemy as **Marked Prey**.

Against Marked Prey, Yautja allies gain:

- +1 attack bonus
- +1 Survival/Track bonus
- +1 Will save bonus against fear or confusion caused by that prey

This lasts until the prey is defeated, escapes, or the encounter ends.

**Typical Equipment Access:**

**Allowed:**

- Command-grade bio-mask
- Pack-level communication network
- Advanced ship systems
- Heavy plasma weapon access
- Ceremonial command weapon
- Authority over hunting grounds

**Roleplay Notes:**

A Pack Leader must balance glory with responsibility. A reckless Pack Leader gets hunters killed. A cowardly one loses all respect.

## Levels 14–17: Clan Leader

Clan Leaders are rulers, warlords, judges, and living symbols of their lineage. They command more than hunts; they command history.

**Benefits:**

- Natural Armor improves to +6
- Damage Reduction improves to 6/-
- Hunter’s Patience insight bonus improves to +4
- +4 bonus on Intimidate, Knowledge: Yautja Culture, and Survival
- May command multiple packs
- May declare a Blood Hunt against a Bad Blood, abomination, or clan enemy
- May grant or revoke access to restricted clan technology

**Clan Authority:**

Once per session, a Clan Leader may call upon clan resources, subject to story circumstances.

**Examples:**

- A hunting vessel
- A squad of lesser Yautja
- Ancient weaponry
- A captured xenomorph specimen
- Restricted data from old hunts
- Ritual judgment
- Medical restoration
- A trial by combat

**Typical Equipment Access:**

**Allowed:**

- Masterwork Yautja weapons
- Ancient clan relics
- High-grade cloak systems
- Heavy armor
- Command vessels
- Restricted xenomorph containment technology
- Clan archives

**Roleplay Notes:**

Clan Leaders are not merely powerful. They embody a philosophy of the hunt. Their decisions shape generations.

## Levels 18–20: Elder

Elders are mythic figures among the Yautja. Many are centuries old. Some have hunted humans, xenomorphs, Engineers, synthetics, abominations, and things no human archive has named.

**Benefits:**

- Natural Armor improves to +7
- Damage Reduction improves to 8/-
- Hunter’s Patience insight bonus improves to +5
- +6 Reputation bonus among Yautja
- Immune to ordinary fear effects
- +4 bonus on Will saves against mind-affecting effects
- May judge entire clans, end feuds, or sanction legendary hunts

**Elder's Judgment:**

Once per session, an Elder may declare a formal judgment.

**Possible effects:**

- A dishonored Yautja is marked as Bad Blood.
- A young hunter is elevated early due to extraordinary glory.
- A forbidden hunt is sanctioned.
- A clan debt is invoked.
- A relic weapon is released from the archives.
- A defeated enemy is spared as worthy.
- A human, synthetic, or alien outsider is recognized as honorable prey, ally, or blooded exception.

**Typical Equipment Access:**

**Allowed:**

- Ancient relic weapons
- Elder-grade bio-mask
- Unique hunting armor
- Lost clan technology
- Personal warship or command vessel
- Access to the deepest clan records
- Authority over life, death, exile, and honor

**Roleplay Notes:**

An Elder should feel like a final boss, mentor, judge, or legend. At this level, the Yautja is not just a hunter. They are a walking myth.

## Suggested Starting Yautja Weapons

These can be refined later into full d20 Future stat blocks.

## Wrist Blades

**Damage:** 1d8 slashing  
**Critical:** 19–20  
**Damage Type:** Slashing  
**Size:** Light melee weapon  
**Special:** Retractable; cannot be disarmed while attached; grants +2 bonus to conceal weapon.

## Combi-Stick

**Damage:** 1d10 piercing/slashing  
**Critical:** x3  
**Size:** Two-handed or one-handed for Yautja  
**Special:** Collapsible; may be thrown; can be used as spear or staff.

## Smart Disc / Fan Disc

**Damage:** 2d6 slashing  
**Critical:** 19–20  
**Range Increment:** 30 ft.  
**Special:** Returns to wielder on a successful attack roll or programmed path; requires Yautja training.

## Netgun

**Damage:** Special  
**Range Increment:** 20 ft.  
**Effect:** Target must make a Reflex save or become entangled/pinned.  
**Special:** Against unarmored or lightly armored targets, constriction may deal slashing damage each round.

## Plasma Caster

**Damage:** 3d8 fire/energy  
**Critical:** x2  
**Range Increment:** 80 ft.  
**Special:** Shoulder-mounted; requires bio-mask targeting; may be charged for greater damage at the cost of a full-round action.

## Yautja Weaknesses and Limitations

These help balance the species.

## Heat Dependency

Yautja prefer warm climates. In cold environments, they suffer a **-2 penalty on Fortitude saves against cold exposure** unless wearing thermal mesh, armor, or environmental gear.

## Bio-Mask Reliance

Without a bio-mask, a Yautja loses access to enhanced visual modes, targeting support, translation, and many weapon interfaces.

The GM may impose:

- -2 attack penalty with plasma caster
- Inability to use certain smart weapons
- Reduced Spot/Search effectiveness
- Difficulty reading human displays or fine visual data

## Honor Restrictions

A Yautja who violates the Code may lose access to:

- Rank benefits
- Prestige classes
- Clan equipment
- Reputation bonuses
- Command authority

## Reputation Cuts Both Ways

A Yautja’s fame can attract challengers, corporate hunters, xenomorph cultists, Bad Bloods, rival clans, or Weyland-Yutani acquisition teams.

## Character Creation Summary

When creating a Yautja PC:

1. Choose heroic class normally.
2. Apply Yautja racial traits.
3. Begin as **Unblooded** if level 1–3.
4. Choose allowed starting gear based on classification.
5. Define clan, hunting philosophy, and first intended trophy.
6. Decide whether the Yautja is traditional, experimental, merciful, brutal, spiritual, technological, or borderline Bad Blood.

## Recommended Clan Questions

To make each Yautja distinct, answer:

1. What prey does your clan consider most sacred?
2. Does your clan favor old weapons or advanced technology?
3. Are humans worthy prey, vermin, allies, or curiosities?
4. How does your clan view xenomorphs?
5. What act would make a hunter Bad Blood?
6. What trophy does your character most desire?
7. What shame or unfinished hunt haunts them?
8. Who trained them?
9. What weapon did they earn first?
10. What would they refuse to kill?
